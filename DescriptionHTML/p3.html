Ingredients:

  • 1/2 cup (115g) unsalted butter
  • 6 oz (170g) semi-sweet chocolate (chopped)
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 large egg yolks
  • 1/4 cup (50g) granulated sugar
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1/4 cup (30g) all-purpose flour

Instructions:

  1. Preheat and Prep: Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C). Grease 4 ramekins with butter and dust lightly with cocoa powder or flour.
  2. Melt Chocolate and Butter: In a microwave-safe bowl, melt butter and chocolate together in 20-second intervals, stirring each time, until smooth.
  3. Mix Wet Ingredients: In another bowl, whisk eggs, egg yolks, sugar, and salt until slightly thickened and pale.
  4. Bake: Pour the batter into ramekins and place them on a baking sheet. Bake for 12-14 minutes until the edges are set, but the center remains soft.
  5. Serve: Let rest for 1 minute, then run a knife around the edges and invert onto a plate. Serve immediately with ice cream or berries.
